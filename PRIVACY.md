# Ghost Runner privacy policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026  
Owner: Ayaan Khan

Ghost Runner replays running workouts on a map. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Health data

With your permission, Ghost Runner reads running workouts and their associated workout routes from Apple Health. It uses this information to show your runs, draw their routes, and replay a route with a moving marker.

Ghost Runner has read-only access to Apple Health. It never writes, changes, or deletes Health data.

You can revoke Health access at any time in **Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Ghost Runner**.

## Data storage and sharing

Ghost Runner stores no copies of Health data. It reads workouts and routes from Apple Health each time you run the app. Health data stays on your device. Revoking Health access stops all reads. To delete the underlying workout or route, delete it in the Health app.

Ghost Runner stores local preferences, such as your unit choice, playback speed, and welcome-guide status, in UserDefaults on your device. Deleting Ghost Runner removes those preferences.

Imported GPX runs are copied into Ghost Runner's local app storage, stay on your device, and can be deleted from the Runs tab.

Ghost Runner has no account system, analytics, third-party data sharing, or advertising. It does not send your Health data to Ayaan Khan or to any other party.

Map tiles come from Apple's MapKit. Apple processes MapKit data under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Ghost Runner sends no data to Apple beyond what MapKit needs to show the map.
